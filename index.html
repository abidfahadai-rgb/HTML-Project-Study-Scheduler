About the Application

The Study Scheduler is a lightweight web application made to help university students keep their academic life in order. From assignment deadlines to revision sessions, every task finds its proper place.

The goal is to feel less like a noisy to-do list and more like a personal almanac kept on your desk.

Key Features

  • Login system with personal accounts
  • Add, edit and delete study tasks
  • Course code, due date, priority, and notes
  • Overdue tasks highlighted in red
  • Filter tasks by subject, status or date
  • Live clock at the bottom of every page
